◆ Teaching Points
Subnetting: radios on different networks (e.g. 192.168.1.x vs 192.168.2.x, or mismatched masks) can't talk even sitting side by side — Layer 3 blocks them. Placement: mesh links only form within range; a node placed too far is isolated, but an in-range relay can bridge the gap. Missing nodes: in a chain, powering off the middle relay partitions the network — nodes on each side keep local links but lose the far side unless an alternate path exists. Redundant placement (multiple paths) survives a lost node; a single-path chain does not.
