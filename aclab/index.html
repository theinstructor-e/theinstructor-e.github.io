TRANSFORMER — LIVE MODEL
▶ HOW TO USE
Enter values for any three of the four fields (primary turns, secondary turns, primary voltage, secondary voltage) — the calculator solves for whichever one you leave on "auto." Click Lock next to a field to keep it fixed while you adjust the others, or click a field's lock again to release it.
