What is Wi-Fi?
Every version explained!

Wi-Fi is the invisible signal that lets your tablet, phone, and computer talk to the internet — no wires needed! Here's how it got faster and smarter over the years.

2.4 GHz — The long-range band
Goes through walls better, but shared with microwaves & baby monitors — can get crowded!
Frequency 2.4 GHz 2,400,000,000 waves per second — lower means longer waves that travel farther
Range indoors ~45 metres Punches through walls, floors and furniture better than any other Wi-Fi band
Typical real speed 20–150 Mbps Enough for video calls and HD streaming, but gets slower when many devices are connected
Min speed 1 Mbps The original 802.11 standard from 1997 — barely faster than a dial-up phone line
Max speed 600 Mbps 802.11n (Wi-Fi 4) is the fastest standard that uses 2.4 GHz — though real-world speeds are much lower
Crowding problem High Shared with microwaves, baby monitors, Bluetooth and neighbouring routers — only 3 non-overlapping channels available
Best for Far-away devices Smart TVs in back bedrooms, devices through multiple walls, smart home gadgets that need a wide reach
⚡
5 GHz — The fast band
Much faster speeds, less crowded, but doesn't travel as far through walls.
Frequency 5 GHz 5,000,000,000 waves per second — shorter waves carry more data but lose energy faster
Range indoors ~15–25 metres Walls and floors noticeably reduce the signal — works best in the same room or one wall away
Typical real speed 100–900 Mbps Much faster than 2.4 GHz when you're close enough — great for gaming and 4K streaming
Min speed 54 Mbps 802.11a from 1999 was the first 5 GHz standard — fast for its time but basic by today's standards
Max speed 46 Gbps 802.11be (Wi-Fi 7) across all bands — the 5 GHz portion alone can hit around 5 Gbps in ideal conditions
Crowding problem Medium 24 non-overlapping channels available — far less congested than 2.4 GHz, especially in apartments
Best for Speed near the router Laptops, gaming consoles, and phones that are in the same room or nearby — when speed matters most
🚀
6 GHz — The brand new band
Only the very newest Wi-Fi uses this. Blazing fast and barely any interference!
Frequency 6 GHz 6,000,000,000 waves per second — brand new to Wi-Fi, only unlocked by regulators in 2020
Range indoors ~10–15 metres Shortest range of the three bands — works best in open spaces or the same room as the router
Typical real speed 1–4 Gbps Blazing fast when close by — enough to download a full HD film in under 10 seconds
Min speed ~600 Mbps Wi-Fi 6E entry-level devices in real-world conditions — still faster than most home broadband connections
Max speed 46 Gbps Wi-Fi 7 theoretical maximum using all three bands together — the 6 GHz portion alone can reach around 23 Gbps
Crowding problem Very low Opens up 1,200 MHz of fresh spectrum with 59 channels — older devices cannot use it, so it is never congested
Best for Ultra-fast nearby VR headsets, wireless monitors, cloud gaming, and any device that needs maximum speed with minimum lag
