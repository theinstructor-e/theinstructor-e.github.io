J/S = ERP_j - PathLoss_j - S_target + BW_factor [dB]
Burn-through range: R_bt = R_j / sqrt(J/S_ratio)
Spot jam: all power on one freq, max J/S | Barrage: power spread, lower J/S per channel
Deceptive jamming: lower power, harder to detect than noise jamming
BURN-THROUGH: At close enough range, the target signal overpowers the jamming. J/S > 0dB = jamming effective. J/S > 20dB = effective denial. Below 0dB = burn-through, signal is readable. Jammer must maintain geometric advantage to stay above burn-through range.
