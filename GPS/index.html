EW Indicators & Warnings — Key Observations
⚡ GPS Jamming
Jamming broadcasts a high-power noise signal on L1 (1575.42 MHz) that overwhelms the very weak GPS satellite signals (~-130 dBm). The receiver's noise floor rises, the C/N₀ ratio collapses, and the receiver loses lock entirely.

Effect on target: Navigation fails. Drone activates failsafe — usually RTH (Return To Home), loiter, or crash depending on configuration.

EW operator advantage: Jamming is detectable. The receiver knows it lost lock. Operators on both sides can detect the jammer location via DF. A visible effect tells the enemy they're being jammed.
⚠ GPS Spoofing
Spoofing transmits convincing but false GPS signals that gradually replace the real ones. The receiver believes it's locked — C/N₀ looks normal, satellite count looks normal — but it's navigating to a phantom location.

Effect on target: Drone thinks it's on route but is actually flying somewhere else entirely. Can be used to walk a drone into a kill zone, or land it in controlled territory.

EW operator advantage: Spoofing is invisible to the target. No warning flags trip. No failsafe activates. The target has no idea it's been deceived. Far harder to defend against than jamming.
