Warrior Tasks \u2014 5 subject areas
Warrior tasks are individual Soldier skills known to be critical to survival, organized into five subject areas (examples below \u2014 not the full list).
COUNTS VARY: editions differ (e.g. ~39 tasks in 2011; 65 tasks + 62 drills in others). Treat the 5-area framework as solid; confirm the exact task list against the current publication.
Battle Drills \u2014 click one to run the simulation
A battle drill is a collective action executed by a platoon or smaller element without a deliberate decision-making process \u2014 rehearsed until instinctive.

WARRIOR TASK vs BATTLE DRILL: a warrior task is what the individual Soldier does (load a weapon, apply a tourniquet); a battle drill is what the team/squad does together on a cue.
FOOM \u2014 Formations and Order of Movement
FOOM covers how leaders organize and maneuver their teams. Formation choice is driven by METT-TC \u2014 it balances firepower, control, and security. Reference: TC 3-21.76 (Ranger Handbook), ATP 3-21.8.
