Warrior Tasks \u2014 5 subject areas
Warrior tasks are individual Soldier skills known to be critical to survival. They are organized into five subject areas (examples below \u2014 not the full list).
COUNTS VARY: different editions list different totals (e.g., ~39 in 2011; 65 warrior tasks + 62 battle drills in other editions). Treat the 5-area framework as solid and confirm the exact task list against the current publication.
Battle Drills \u2014 collective, rehearsed reactions
A battle drill is a collective action executed by a platoon or smaller element without a deliberate decision-making process \u2014 rehearsed until it is instinctive. Common examples:
WARRIOR TASK vs BATTLE DRILL: a warrior task is what the individual Soldier can do (load a weapon, apply a tourniquet); a battle drill is what the team/squad does together on a cue (react to contact, react to ambush).
