HOW IT WORKS

RSSI HOMING

Signal strength falls as you move away, so you can walk toward "hotter" readings. It is slow, and buildings, trees, and fading make it jump around.

THE ARRAY AND TIME DIFFERENCE OF ARRIVAL

A radio wave reaches each antenna on the pack at a slightly different instant. For antennas half a meter apart that is at most about 1.7 nanoseconds. The SDR measures these tiny differences as phase between its coherent channels. With known antenna positions it solves for the direction the wave came from: that is one line of bearing. Two antennas leave a front/back ambiguity. Four resolve it.

WHY SPACING AND FREQUENCY MATTER

Phase only tells you the time difference modulo one wave period. If any two antennas are more than half a wavelength apart, the measurement wraps and the bearing is wrong. If they are far less than a wavelength apart, noise and calibration errors swamp the tiny difference.

FIXING THE EMITTER

One line of bearing points toward the emitter. Lines taken from different places cross at the emitter. Lines that cross at a shallow angle give a poor fix, and reflections off buildings can bend a line.

TRUE TDOA GEOLOCATION

Real TDOA geolocation uses several time-synchronized sensors spread over distance. Each pair of sensors gives a hyperbola of constant time difference, and the hyperbolas intersect at the emitter. A single pack uses the same time difference idea across a tiny array to get a bearing. The DF Trainer covers fixes from several sensors.

REAL SYSTEMS (PUBLIC SOURCES)

  • KrakenSDR: a 5-channel coherent SDR doing correlative interferometry and MUSIC, used with a phone for GPS and compass.
  • R&S MP007: man-portable tactical DF, 9 MHz to 8 GHz, about 2° RMS typical.
  • SwRI AP-460 “tent”: a collapsible fabric DF array that stows in a backpack, set up in under 5 minutes, better than 5° RMS.
  • JTWS man-portable: backpack system with VHF/UHF DF capability.
  • TCI 903S: man-portable COMINT and DF.
  • Keysight 85574A: handheld DF antenna with digital compass for manual bearings.
  • Ham TDOA homers: two-antenna switched arrays that you sweep to find the direction.
