▶ HOW TO READ IT
Time scrolls downward — newest at the top. Width on the display is bandwidth. Gaps in time mean the emitter isn't transmitting continuously. A signal whose position jumps around between frames is hopping frequency. Watch a few seconds before answering — duty cycle and hopping behavior only show up over time, not in a single instant.
