SETTINGS
Mode and wind are set by the lesson. They are free in Free Flight.
CONTROLS

Mode 2 layout. Left stick: up and down is throttle (it stays where you leave it), left and right is yaw (turn). Right stick: up and down is pitch (forward and back), left and right is roll (slide left and right). The right stick springs back to center.

KeyboardW / S throttle · A / D yaw · Arrow keys pitch and roll · Space arm · R reset · C camera · M mode
TouchDrag the two stick pads. Use FULLSCREEN to play with the sticks over the picture.
GamepadIf a gamepad or USB radio is connected, the sticks follow it (left: yaw and throttle, right: roll and pitch).
