One-third, two-thirds planning time
Leaders should use about one third of the available time for their own planning and leave at least two thirds for subordinates. Enter when the mission arrives and when it must be executed.

COA screening and decision matrix
Screen each course of action, then score it against your evaluation criteria. Criteria and weights below are examples. Change them to fit the mission.
STEP 3: a valid COA is Suitable, Feasible, Acceptable, Distinguishable, and Complete. Uncheck any test a COA fails.
STEP 5: weight each criterion from 1 (minor) to 5 (critical). Score each COA from 1 (poor) to 5 (best) on each criterion.
A MATRIX SUPPORTS JUDGMENT: the numbers are opinions. If the leader changes when one weight moves by a point, that criterion deserves a closer look and a conversation with the commander.
SOURCES FM 5-0, Planning and Orders Production (2022), and ADP 5-0, The Operations Process. The criteria and weights in the matrix are examples, not doctrine. See the guide for details.
