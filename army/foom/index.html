Bounding overwatch — alternate versus successive
Two elements, A and B, cross the ground. Only one moves at a time while the other overwatches. Step through both methods side by side.
Alternate bounds — front of unit
—
Successive bounds — front of unit
—
ALTERNATE: the rear element moves past the lead element and takes up overwatch. Usually faster. SUCCESSIVE: the lead element advances, then the rear element moves up to about even with it. Easier to control, but slower. In both, only one element moves at a time, and the overwatch element covers from a covered, concealed position.
SOURCES ATP 3-21.8, Infantry Platoon and Squad, and TC 3-21.76, Ranger Handbook. The scenarios are written for training. See the guide for details.
