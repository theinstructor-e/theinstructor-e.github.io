THE SEQUENCE
  1. Mate the antenna base with ANT and hand-tighten. Plug the handset into AUD/DATA, lining up the keys.
  2. Turn FCTN to TST and wait for GOOD.
  3. Turn FCTN to LD.
  4. MENU: CHAN to 2 (press 2), PWR to M, MODE to SC, CMSC to PT (CHG changes PWR, MODE, and CMSC).
  5. Press FREQ, CLR (MENU/CLR), key in 40500, and STO within 7 seconds.
  6. Turn FCTN to SQ ON.
  7. Radio check: callsigns, say what you want, over. Acknowledge, out.
